6. "Automated Interview System using Speech Analysis" (Li & Chen, 2023) To simulate technical interviews and reduce student anxiety using voice agents. Used Speech-to-Text (STT) and NLP to analyze candidate spoken responses for sentiment and keyword accuracy. Reduced candidate anxiety by 40% before real interviews; provided immediate feedback on confidence. High latency in voice processing; struggled with heavy accents; ignored non-verbal cues. Validates our Voice-Interactive Interview module. We improve on this by using Gemini 1.5 Flash for lower latency interactions.
7. "Skill Gap Analysis using Ontology Mapping" (Rao et al., 2022) To recommend learning paths based on specific deficits in a student's resume. Used Collaborative Filtering on course data to map resume keywords to missing standard skills. Accurate recommendation of standard technical courses (e.g., recommend SQL if Database is missing). Suffered from "Cold Start" problem; could not handle new niche modern technologies dynamically. Supports our "Skill Gap Analysis" feature. We use Generative AI to solve the Cold Start problem, allowing instant recommendations for any tech.
8. "Bias Mitigation in AI Recruitment" (Wilson, 2021) To identify and remove gender or demographic bias in automated CV parsing systems. Tested BERT models on anonymized vs. non-anonymized datasets to measure scoring variance. Found that standard models retained bias towards male-dominated keywords even without names. Anonymization reduced the accuracy of context detection; hard to balance fairness and accuracy. Highlights the need for our "Explainable AI". We explicitly show *why* a score is low (e.g., "Missing Java"), preventing hidden bias.
9. "Scalability of E-Governance Portals using Distributed SQL" (Kumar, 2024) To handle massive traffic spikes during result declaration and placement drive registrations. Compared traditional MySQL performance vs. TiDB and CockroachDB under simulated high load. Distributed SQL maintained 100% uptime and data consistency while MySQL crashed under concurrent writes. Higher infrastructure complexity and initial setup time compared to single-node databases. Directly justifies our architectural choice of TiDB Serverless to ensure the portal never crashes during placement drives.
10. "Gamification in Engineering Education" (Suresh & Patel, 2023) To improve student engagement in non-academic career preparation activities. Implemented leaderboards, badges, and point systems for completing extra-curricular tasks. Observed a 60% increase in voluntary student participation and daily logins. Students sometimes focused on "gaming the system" for points rather than actual learning quality. Supports our "Leaderboard" feature. We mitigate the limitation by tying points strictly to verified test scores, not just participation.
